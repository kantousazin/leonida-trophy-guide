Leonida Trophy Guide — an offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: a checklist saved in your browser with export and import, filters and a spoiler setting, a roadmap to the platinum, the missable trophies flagged, and a guide to every trophy in our own words with its sources cited.

## Run it

```sh
node --experimental-strip-types main.ts
# or serve the app directly:
python3 -m http.server --directory app 8000
```
