Roadmap to the Platinum

Five phases, built only from what the official trophy descriptions actually say. Story and chapter trophies come first because strands, activities and counters open up as Leonida opens up. Cumulative trophies (250 stunts, 100 WAiNK locations, $250,000 laundered) are started in the early phases and finished in the late ones — the single most common platinum mistake is leaving them all for the end. Missables & one-time risks

Honesty note: Grand Theft Auto VI was not yet released when this guide was written, so no trophy can be confirmed missable. The flags below are precautionary readings of the official wording — trophies whose description describes a single moment, an opening mission, or a succeed-on-the-retry pattern rather than a repeatable counter. Everything not flagged is a repeatable activity, a cumulative counter, or a completion reward on the wording given. Treat the flagged four as “secure these when the moment comes, and keep a save”, not as proven missables.

Save habits that cover every risk on this list

  • Keep a manual save before each chapter break and before the final mission.
  • Keep an early save until the opening-mission trophy (Just Like Paradise) has popped.
  • When the story takes you to VCIA or Ocean Beach, explore the moment before moving on.
  • Retry a failed activity at least once instead of always reloading — one trophy reads as a second-attempt reward.
